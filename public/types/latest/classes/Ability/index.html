    • toDB(): DBAbility

      Returns the document-store representation of this object.

      Caution

      Unlike toJSON(), the resulting document-store representation may be in a significantly different shape to that of its implementing class, and it cannot be passed back into the constructor to reconstruct an equivalent instance.

      Use fromDB() instead, which reconstructs the instance and rehydrates any fields truncated by the conversion.
